# Requirements: Tarot Foundations Landing Page

**Defined:** 2026-10-29
**Core Value:** Visitors should understand what the course offers and feel confident making an informed decision without relying on fabricated outcomes or social proof.

## v1 Requirements

### Credibility

- [ ] **TRUST-01**: The page uses only approved or verifiable course, mentor, and student-proof claims.
- [ ] **TRUST-02**: The page omits unsupported student counts, star ratings, scarcity claims, and guaranteed outcomes.
- [ ] **TRUST-03**: The page clearly explains the course scope and price before a visitor joins.

### Testimonials

- [ ] **TEST-01**: The six approved student videos are available in a continuously scrolling horizontal carousel.
- [ ] **TEST-02**: The carousel pauses during focus, interaction, or video playback and remains usable with reduced motion.

### Responsive Experience

- [ ] **RESP-01**: The landing page works at mobile widths without horizontal page overflow.
- [ ] **RESP-02**: Mobile animations remain performant and respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Conversion

- [ ] **CONV-01**: Course calls to action lead to the on-page ₹599 pricing section until an external checkout URL is provided.

## v2 Requirements

### Checkout

- **CHECK-01**: Course calls to action can link directly to the live payment checkout after the owner supplies and verifies its URL.
- **MEAS-01**: Checkout-start and purchase events can be measured once an analytics setup is selected.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Invented student quotes, identities, or star ratings | Would undermine the requested genuine, trustworthy presentation |
| Guaranteed income or learning outcomes | Results depend on the learner and no guarantee was provided |
| New payment provider integration | No checkout URL or payment-provider requirements were provided |
