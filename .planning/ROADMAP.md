# Roadmap: Tarot Foundations Landing Page

## Overview

One focused phase improves credibility, testimonial presentation, and mobile polish on the existing static landing page.

## Phases

### Phase 1: Credible Mobile Landing Page

**Goal:** Visitors can understand the course and its price, review approved student experiences, and use the page comfortably on mobile without unsupported proof claims.

**Requirements:** TRUST-01, TRUST-02, TRUST-03, TEST-01, TEST-02, RESP-01, RESP-02, CONV-01

**Success criteria:**
1. The page shows the course scope, ₹599 price, and owner-approved 10 years of experience without unsupported enrollment, rating, scarcity, or outcome claims.
2. The six approved testimonial videos are presented in a continuous horizontal loop; it pauses for keyboard focus, touch interaction, and video playback.
3. Reduced-motion mode disables automatic movement while keeping testimonials reachable by horizontal scrolling.
4. At mobile viewport widths, the page has no horizontal document overflow and primary actions remain visible and usable.
5. Course CTAs continue to point to the on-page pricing section until a checkout URL is supplied.

**Plans:** To be created with `/gsd-plan-phase 1`.
